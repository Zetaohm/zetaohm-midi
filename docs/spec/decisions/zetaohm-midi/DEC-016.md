# DEC-016 — BLE-MIDI in a new sans-IO crate zmidi-ble; the BLE host adapter is separate

**Status:** accepted

BLE-MIDI (Specification for MIDI over Bluetooth Low Energy, v1.0) goes in a new workspace crate
`zmidi-ble`, the eighth specdev member (library-api). It is `no_std` with no `alloc` (DEC-002),
synchronous and sans-IO (DEC-004): it has no BLE stack dependency, and the caller's BLE host moves
the bytes of the MIDI Data I/O characteristic.

Scope of `zmidi-ble`:
- The GATT data of section 3: the MIDI Service and MIDI Data I/O Characteristic UUIDs and the
  characteristic properties, as constants for the BLE host.
- The packet codec of sections 7 to 9: header and timestamp bytes, full and Running Status
  messages, System Real-Time messages, System Exclusive across packets (continuation packets), and
  the 13-bit millisecond timestamps with the wrap rule.
- The reader gives UMPs (with the 13-bit timestamp of each) through the MIDI 1.0 byte stream codec
  of `zmidi-io`; the writer takes UMPs through the same codec and fills packets of at most the
  negotiated ATT MTU minus 3 bytes.

`zmidi-ble` depends on `zmidi-ump` and `zmidi-io`; `midi` re-exports it.

The BLE host adapter (`trouble-host`, DEC-003) is a separate Cargo crate, not a specdev member, as
the USB adapters are (DEC-015). It registers the service, answers the first read with an empty
payload (section 5), asks for a connection interval of 15 ms or less (section 4), does the MTU
exchange (section 6) and connects the codec to the characteristic. It contains no protocol logic
(DEC-004).

This decision replaces the BLE parts of DEC-003 (BLE-MIDI GATT service and packet codec in
`zmidi-io`) and of DEC-004 (BLE-MIDI packets codec in `zmidi-io`). The external BLE host stays
`trouble-host` (DEC-003). The UART parts of DEC-003 and DEC-004 do not change.

## Context

Roadmap section 4. BLE-MIDI starts with the spec work for a new member; the decision follows the
pattern of DEC-014 and DEC-015 for USB-MIDI.
BLE-MIDI v1.0 carries the MIDI 1.0 byte stream only (Preface: no virtual cables, no clock
synchronization), so the UMP side needs the MIDI 1.0 byte stream codec of `zmidi-io`.

## Consequences

- The workspace gets an eighth specdev member with its own spec.toml, specs/ and gates, including a
  bare-metal gate (DEC-002).
- Catalogue rows of BLE-MIDI v1.0 have the owner `zmidi-ble`; the GATT server, advertising, pairing,
  the connection interval and the MTU exchange are `device` (the BLE host and the adapter).
- The adapter crate comes after the codec. It adds `trouble-host` as a dependency; its version and
  API need their own decision.
- BLE-MIDI Running Status differs from the MIDI 1.0 byte stream: System Common does not cancel it,
  and the end of a packet does. The reader expands Running Status itself and gives `zmidi-io` whole
  messages only.

## Alternatives

- BLE-MIDI in `zmidi-io`, as DEC-003 said: rejected. One crate for each specification document
  (DEC-006, DEC-012, DEC-014), and `zmidi-io` stays free of transports.
- The adapter as a Cargo feature of `zmidi-ble`: rejected for the same reason as in DEC-014: the
  codec crate stays free of stack dependencies.
- An own BLE-MIDI to UMP converter with no `zmidi-io`: rejected. It repeats the System Exclusive
  splitter and the MIDI 1.0 to MIDI 2.0 conversion of `zmidi-io`.

## Verification

`zmidi-ble` builds for the bare-metal target with `--no-default-features` and has no dependency on
`trouble-host`, `bt-hci` or a HAL crate. The packet figures of BLE-MIDI v1.0 sections 7 and 8 are
test vectors.
